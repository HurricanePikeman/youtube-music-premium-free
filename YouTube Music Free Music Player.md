### 1️⃣ Uninstall the original app
If you have the official YouTube Music app installed, you must uninstall it first to avoid signature conflicts.

### 2️⃣ Install MicroG (if required)
Some versions require MicroG to handle Google account logins. Download and install MicroG first.

### 3️⃣ Install the Mod APK
Download the latest APK from the button at the top, tap to install, and log in with your Google account.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

<details>
<summary><b>Is this legal?</b></summary>

This is a modified client (mod APK). It violates the Terms of Service of YouTube and Google, but it is heavily used globally as a privacy and accessibility tool. It is intended for **educational and personal use**. We highly recommend supporting artists by buying merchandise or concert tickets.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Do I need root access?</b></summary>

No. The standard APK installs just like any other app. You only need root if you want to patch the official app directly in place, but using the pre-patched APK requires no root.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Will I get banned?</b></summary>

There have been no widespread reports of account bans for using music mods. However, Google can technically terminate accounts. Use a "burner" account if you are paranoid about your main Google account.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>How do I update the app?</b></summary>

Because this is not on the Play Store, you must download the new APK manually when a new version is released. Your playlists and library will sync automatically once you log in.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Why do I need MicroG?</b></summary>

MicroG is an open-source replacement for Google Play Services. The mod APK uses it to authenticate your Google account and load your playlists without needing the heavy background processes of the official Play Services.
</details>
